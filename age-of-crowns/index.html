How to play

Economy

Select villagers and right-click sheep, berries, trees, gold or stone to gather. Build a Lumber Camp, Mining Camp or Mill next to resources so villagers walk less. Farms give endless food once the hunt runs out.

Houses each support 5 population. Advance through the Feudal, Castle and Imperial Ages at the Town Center once you own two buildings of the current age.

Victory

Destroy every enemy unit and building, or build a Wonder and keep it standing for five minutes.

# Age of Crowns — Cheat Codes

## How to enter them

- **Phone or tablet:** tap **Menu** (top right), open **Cheats**, and tap any cheat. You can
  also type a code in the box under the list and tap **Use**.
- **Keyboard:** press **Enter** during a game, type the code and press **Enter** again.
  **Esc** closes the box.

Codes ignore capital letters and extra spaces. Cheats only ever affect you; the computer
players never cheat.

| `ninjalui` | +100,000 of every resource |

## Building and progress

| Code | Effect |
|---|---|
| `aegis` | Buildings finish, units train and technologies research instantly, and villagers gather 25× faster. Enter it again to turn it off. |
| `to the future` | Advance to the next age instantly: Dark → Feudal → Castle → Imperial. |

## Map

| Code | Effect |
|---|---|
| `marco` | Reveal the whole map. Enemies stay hidden outside your units' sight. |
| `polo` | Remove the fog of war so you see everything live. Enter it again to bring the fog back. |

## Units

| Code | Effect |
|---|---|
| `how do you turn this on` | Spawns a **Cobra Car** next to your Town Center: a sports car with roof machine guns. It is fast (2.6 speed), tough (600 HP, 8/8 armor) and fires bursts that also wreck buildings. Enter it as many times as you like. |
| `big army` | Spawns 6 knights and 6 archers (or their upgraded versions) next to your Town Center. |
| `natural wonders` | Every wild animal becomes yours: sheep, deer, boar and wolves. Tamed wolves fight for you and leave your people alone. |

## Winning and losing

| Code | Effect |
|---|---|
| `black death` | All enemy players are destroyed instantly. |
| `torpedo2` … `torpedo8` | Destroy a single player by number (the number is their slot in the player list; you are 1). |
| `i r winner` | Win immediately. |
| `wimpywimpywimpy` | Give up and lose. |

## Fun combinations

- **Sandbox mode:** `ninjalui` + `aegis` + `to the future` (three times). You then have every
  building, unit and upgrade instantly with endless resources.
- **Car chase:** enter `how do you turn this on` a few times, select the cars and right-click an
  enemy Town Center.
- **Wolf pack:** on the *Frozen North* or *Black Forest* maps, enter `natural wonders` early. The
  wolf packs become yours; send them at the enemy villagers.
